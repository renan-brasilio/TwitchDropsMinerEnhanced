Twitch Authentication
Tip for Seamless Login: You can enter your Twitch auth-token directly. To get it:
  1. Log into twitch.tv in your browser.
  2. Press F12 (or Cmd+Option+I on Mac) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Go to Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox/Safari) → Cookies → https://www.twitch.tv.
  4. Find the cookie named auth-token and copy its value.
